Culture log 01  ·  A theme for T3 Code

Your agent works.
Colonies grow.

A lab-culture theme for T3 Code: notebook paper, amber agar and petri plates in the margins. While a turn runs, colonies spread across the plates. They grow once and hold, however long the turn runs. When it ends, the plates settle at full growth and a GROWTH CHECKED tab stamps onto the composer.

01

Motion that means something.

Every animation in Agar is tied to real app state. If nothing is running, nothing moves, so a glance at the margin tells you whether your agent is still at work.

Detail AGrows once, then holds

Two plates, one culture

On wide windows each gutter holds a petri plate. A nutrient plate grows scattered colonies; a blood-agar plate grows them along a four-sector streak, dense to sparse. Growth runs once over the first minute and then holds, with a few late satellites on long turns, so a long turn never wipes the plate. Between turns you see the finished plates, perfectly still.

Colonies in the sidebar

A background agent is easy to spot: its row sprouts four colonies along the lower edge, one after the other. Hover any thread and a single red colony is picked and ringed.

Detail DComposer

A petri rim, lit on focus

The composer and your prompts carry a double rim, like a plate lid. Focus the composer and the inner rim turns teal. A lab tab on its edge reads INCUBATING during a turn, then GROWTH CHECKED stamps down when it finishes. Try the composer above.

04

Inoculate your app.

One JSON file, no build step. Tested on T3 Code 0.1.11 (nightly).

From the app

  1. Copy or download agar.json.
  2. In T3 Code, open Settings › Appearance › Add theme.
  3. Paste the JSON or choose the file, and add it.
Download agar.json

From the terminal

# fetch the theme, then publish and activate it
curl -fsSL -o agar.json https://joshsaintjacque.github.io/t3code-theme-agar/agar.json
~/.t3/bin/t3 theme set agar.json
